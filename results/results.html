Stretched firewall HA

Dual-site Active/Standby Secure Firewall failover across the East and West sites, tested at a realistic 25 ms inter-site round trip before the design is committed.

2 FTD pairs, split across 2 sites FMCv 10.0.1 · FTDv 7.7 25 ms RTT interconnect TRex + iperf3 under load 2026-09-09

Cross-site stateful failover holds at 25 ms.

A planned failover moves the active unit between sites in about 5 seconds and established sessions survive, with no measurable degradation from 3 ms to 40 ms. The real exposure is not firewall latency. It is the shared interconnect (a full cut puts both pairs into split-brain) and edge-router failure, which blackholes a site until routing detects it. Both have concrete mitigations below.

Findings & recommendations

Confirmed

Latency is not the problem

Planned failover preserved sessions at every latency to 40 ms. The state link, which does cross the WAN, syncs well within Cisco's 250 ms ceiling. 25 ms is a sound design point for Active/Standby.

Risk

One circuit means split-brain

A full interconnect cut drove both pairs active at once. Everything rides one inter-site path today. Buy diverse circuits, or accept and plan for the split-brain window.

Mitigated

Firewall HA won't cover the routers

An abrupt edge-router loss blackholed the site for minutes on default BGP timers. Add fast routing failure detection (aggressive BGP timers, BFD where honored); it cut the outage to ~16 s.

Design note

The state link earns its keep

With the state link cut, the next failover reset every session (T3); with it up, sessions survived (T2). Keep failover and state on separate links so one can fail without the other.

Read these as behavior, not sizing. Tests ran on FTDv in Cisco Modeling Labs, a behavioral stand-in for the Secure Firewall 3100 series. Timings and session counts answer "does it work and where does it break," never "how fast is the firewall." Do not present these numbers as capacity evidence.

Design term: "dual-site failover pairs" / split Active/Standby — not "active/active," which invites confusion with clustering (ruled out at this latency). Full method, wiring, and per-scenario data in the build package and results/.
